## Ecosystem and venue brand assets — 2026-11-17

Authentic existing marks for the ecosystem and venue names appearing in the supplied Slipway overview/backend documents. Thirteen SVG logos plus the official Bags transparent PNG are supplied in `public/assets/brands/`; original source artwork is retained under `brands/originals/`. `brands/manifest.json` records names, filenames, original filenames, source URLs, provenance type, variants, viewBoxes and hashes. No logo paths were drawn or traced.

Twelve of the thirteen SVGs came from the brand’s official website, brand repository or frontend repository. Pump.fun is an existing vector from Cryptologo: the current official site exposed its `pump-logomark.svg` URL, but its asset endpoint refused automated download. Its capsule shape was visually verified against the known live mark; its secondary provenance is recorded explicitly. Bags serves a transparent 1024 × 1024 PNG in its official app and documentation; that original is included as `bags.png`, without presenting it as a vector. The documented venue Pools.trade supplies the thirteenth authentic SVG as a fallback when an all-SVG grid is required.

Every SVG was parsed and sanitized into an inert image: no scripts, foreignObject, external images/resources, event handlers or active animation remain. Original SVGs were also checked for scripts, foreignObject, image elements, event handlers and external resource references. Source path `d` sequences match final SVG path sequences exactly; viewBox proportions are preserved, unnecessary page CSS classes/animation styles removed, and Flap’s CSS fill variable resolved to its existing lime fallback. All marks were rendered and visually inspected together after sanitization. Background transparency is retained; white or black regions intrinsic to a logo are retained.

Current Base branding is its square + lowercase wordmark, as supplied by the official brand kit. Zora’s official orb depends on its gradients and becomes an uninformative circle under a monochrome CSS mask. Original color artwork remains available for all marks. Brands retain their trademarks; these assets identify supported/documented ecosystems and venues and do not establish endorsement or partnership. Solana, BNB Chain and PancakeSwap source guidance asks for unaltered artwork. The site implementation determines final display treatment separately.
